Membership level

Gold Membership

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $750 / year

Description: Our premier tier for established corporate leaders looking to maximize community impact and brand positioning.

Perks:

  • Featured directory listing at the top of relevant categories.
  • Free admission for up to 4 guests at all chamber networking events.
  • Homepage spotlight feature (rotating spot on the chamber home page).
  • VIP invitation to annual executive summits and sponsorship priority.

Silver Membership

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $350/Year

Description: Tailored for growing businesses seeking greater visibility and targeted marketing benefits.

Perks:

  • 25% discount on all event tickets and training sessions.
  • One social media spotlight per year on chamber channels.
  • Homepage spotlight feature (rotating spot on the chamber home page).
  • Choice of 1 advertising banner slot in the monthly newsletter.

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $150/Year

Description: Perfect for small business owners and startups looking for essential exposure and networking opportunities.

Perks:

  • Standard business directory listing.
  • 10% discount on all chamber-sponsored training events.
  • Access to monthly networking breakfasts.
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony assistance.

Non Profit Membership

Non Profit Membership

Cost: Free

Description: Designed specifically for 501(c)(3) organizations looking to connect with the local community.

Perks:

  • Basic directory listing on the chamber website.
  • Access to community networking events and luncheons.
  • Invitation to annual non-profit workshops.
